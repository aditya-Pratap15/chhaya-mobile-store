shield Do you wipe my phone's data or photos?

Never. Unlike brand service centers whose policy is an automatic factory reset, we never wipe customer data during hardware screen, battery, port, or casing swaps. You can even keep your phone locked in maintenance mode during bench testing.

payments Do I need to pay any advance deposit?

Zero advance. We inspect your device under the microscope for ₹0. Once we agree on the quote, we perform the work live on the bench. You only pay after you hold the phone, test touch, camera, and display personally.

We stock OEM-spec Grade-A displays with genuine color accuracy, 120Hz refresh rate, and TrueTone transfer support. You can examine the part side-by-side with your old screen before installation begins.
