## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, technically dependable, and consumer-centric retail and repair service. It marries structural rigor with rapid, responsive retail utility. Established to serve both walk-in consumer device owners and back-of-house inventory technicians, the visual posture avoids hollow corporate abstraction in favor of tactile clarity, transparent hardware readiness, and unambiguous trust cues.

### Aesthetic Paradigm: Modern Technical Utility
The aesthetic draws from high-utility modern engineering interfaces combined with consumer-friendly digital commerce. It pairs clean, neutral structural canvases with high-saturation diagnostic and utility cues:
- **Structural Legibility:** Crisp 1px structural framing, high data density without clutter, and clear information hierarchy.
- **Trust-Signaled Highlights:** Immediate visual validation through physical verification badges ("Pay at Store Only", diagnostic warranty seals, live turnaround trackers).
- **Dual Surface Logic:** Seamlessly operates across customer-facing retail storefronts (bright, welcoming, ultra-legible) and back-office management consoles (deep slate sidebars, condensed metric cards, live ticket feeds).

## Colors

The color palette leverages an engineered blue base alongside high-visibility functional signals. 

### Palette Roles & Balance
- **Primary (`#1A56DB` - Electric Tech Blue):** Primary navigation targets, primary CTAs, active diagnostic flows, and branded interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` - Electric Cyan):** Technical accents, interactive hover rings, micro-data indicators, progress meters, and repair status transitions.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Amber Trust):** Expresses warranty badges, offline verification notices ("Pay at Store Only"), urgent turnaround advisories, and conditional notices.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Dark Slate):** Used for primary typography, authoritative framing, headers, and the high-density administration sidebar.
- **Utility WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`):** Reserved exclusively for instant quote triggers, direct technician chat prompts, and repair-ready notifications.

### Semantic Tiers
- **Surface Canvas (Light):** `#F8FAFC` base backdrop; `#FFFFFF` for primary elevations and cards.
- **Surface Canvas (Admin / Dark Sub-systems):** `#0F172A` deep foundation; `#1E293B` elevated containers and control bars.
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#E2E8F0` for light components; `#334155` for dark controls.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes sharp structural contrast between display titles and operational telemetry.

- **Headlines & Structural Titles (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its friendly yet modern geometric construction. The tightened tracking in display weights (-0.02em) provides punchy product headers and retail value propositions.
- **Labels, Interface Utilities & Numerical Data (`Inter`):** Leveraged for tabular metrics, technical repair specs, IMEI codes, price badges, and status labels. It ensures zero ambiguity between alphanumeric characters during intake logs.
- **Numerical Formatting:** Admin KPIs, live service counter IDs, and currency amounts must always use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent shifting layouts in live-updating tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture is predicated on an 8-point vertical cadence and a multi-tiered horizontal grid tailored for inventory tables, repair timelines, and retail product showcases.

### Grid Foundations
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid with `2rem` (32px) margins and `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Maximum content canvas bounded at 1440px. Admin views expand to 100% viewport width with a fixed `16rem` (256px) navigation rail.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Metric overview cards compress from 4 to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer margin and `1rem` gutters. Product cards reflow into high-density 2-column grids or horizontal ticket strips.

### Spatial Rhythms
- Use `space-xs` (4px) exclusively for nested chip padding, badge icons, and input helper spacing.
- Use `space-sm` (8px) for inline metadata, button icon gaps, and table row cellular spacing.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding, form field vertical stacks, and ticket listing separations.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for card-to-card gaps, modal interior boundaries, and admin filter blocks.
- Use `space-xl` (40px) for separating logical inventory sections and marketing headline blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid approach: low-contrast technical outlines paired with ambient, cold-tinted shadows. This approach prevents the interface from feeling muddy while anchoring operational controls cleanly against light backgrounds.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat `#F8FAFC` fill, zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Operational Cards & Grid Items):** `#FFFFFF` fill with a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border and a micro-diffused ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Dropdowns & Diagnostic Menus):** `1px solid #CBD5E1` border with a clean elevation shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-Over Repair Tickets & Critical Alerts):** Framed in `1px solid #94A3B8` backed by `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Admin Dashboard Dark Sidebar:** Uses flat `#0F172A` with a right border of `1px solid #1E293B`, creating structural distinction without blurring or pseudo-depth.

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, balanced geometry (`roundedness: 2`). Radii scale predictably according to visual containment hierarchy to project both precision-engineered durability and approachable consumer access.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Micro Radii (`0.25rem` / 4px):** In-field chips, small warranty tags, status indicator pills within table cells, and checkboxes.
- **Default Base (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Form inputs, default buttons, repair filter dropdowns, and inventory cell hovers.
- **Container Surfaces (`1rem` / 16px):** Product cards, diagnostic inspection panels, KPI summary blocks, and store location cards.
- **Macro Overlays (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Checkout modals, walk-in repair intake forms, and bottom-sheet drawers on mobile screens.
- **Pill Shapes (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for live status chips (e.g., "Ready for Pickup", "Under Testing") and floating action WhatsApp contact buttons.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#1A56DB` with white text, font weight 600, 8px radius. Active hover elevates to `#1E40AF`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with `1px solid #E2E8F0`, slate `#0F172A` text, hovering to `#F1F5F9`.
- **WhatsApp Direct Action:** Solid `#25D366` with `#FFFFFF` text, `9999px` pill radius, paired with the WhatsApp glyph on the left. High visual priority on consumer product details and instant repair quote triggers.
- **Store-Only Interaction:** Dual-tone styling with an amber alert outline (`1px solid #F59E0B`), warm cream surface (`#FFFBEB`), and bold neutral typography.

### 2. Trust & Diagnostic Badges
- **"Pay at Store Only" Badge:** High-contrast indicator featuring a solid `#FEF3C7` background, `#B45309` bold text, uppercase `label-sm` sizing, and an amber location pin icon.
- **Warranty Verification Badge:** Outlined pill using `#06B6D4` borders, a soft `#ECFEFF` fill, and `#0E7490` text. Accompanied by a shield/check icon indicating 30/90/180-day guarantee levels.
- **Turnaround Speed Tag:** `#F1F5F9` neutral chip with a high-visibility clock icon, communicating express turnaround times (e.g., "30-Min Screen Replacement").

### 3. Cards & Product Modules
- **Retail Inventory Cards:** Elevated on Level 1 depth with a standardized 1:1 hardware viewport at the top. Includes device grade tag (e.g., "Mint - Refurbished"), prominent retail price, clear offline pickup note, and a split dual CTA ("View Details" + "Reserve via WhatsApp").
- **Admin Metric (KPI) Cards:** Minimalist styling with a 16px padding box, top-aligned metadata label (`label-md`), high-contrast tabular numeric callout (`code-metric`), and an inline percentage trend tag.

### 4. Form Inputs & Diagnostic Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** 8px radius, `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, white background. Focus state introduces an electric cyan outer halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)` and a `#1A56DB` border stroke.
- **Device Intake Toggles & Checkboxes:** Custom rounded 4px boxes for physical checks (e.g., "Screen Cracked", "Battery Health < 80%"). Checked states fill with `#1A56DB` and display a crisp white checkmark.

### 5. Repair Status Tracking Component
- Horizontal stepping system (Mobile: vertical) tracking intake progression: `Intake Logged` → `Diagnosing` → `Awaiting Parts` → `Bench Testing` → `Ready at Store`.
- Active state pulses with `#06B6D4`, completed steps lock to `#1A56DB`, and pending items remain `#E2E8F0` with slate typography.
